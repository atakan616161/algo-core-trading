AlgoCore Trading

Open-source algorithmic trading toolkit — backtesting, strategy templates, and deployment guides.

What is this?

AlgoCore Trading provides example strategies, a backtesting harness, and adapters to help you prototype algorithmic trading systems in JavaScript/TypeScript.
